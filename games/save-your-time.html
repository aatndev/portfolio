Save your Time

Play on itch.io

Overview

  • Platform: Web Browser (HTML5)
  • Controls: Move (A / D), Jump (Space), Dash (Left Shift), Sword (K)
  • Genre: 2D/3D Parkour Adventure
  • Event: Submission to Mini Jame Gam #58

Game Goal

Reach the end without moving too much.

Your time decreases whenever you are moving. If you fall off the platform or run out of time, you respawn at the start.
