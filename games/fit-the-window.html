Fit the Window

Github (Source Code) Play on itch.io

Overview

  • Platform: Web Browser (HTML5) & Windows
  • Input Controls: Mouse Only (Move & Scroll wheel to Scale)
  • Engine: Unity 3D with C#
  • Genre: Endless / Precision Scaling

How to Play

Move and scroll the mouse to control the window's position and scale.

Fit through the approaching holes without colliding with obstacles. The larger the scale, the higher your score multiplier!
